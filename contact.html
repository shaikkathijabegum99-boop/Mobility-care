COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You Get in Touch

Here are a few common questions about contacting MobilityCare and getting product assistance.

Can you help me choose the right wheelchair?

Yes. Tell us about how and where the wheelchair will be used, and we can help you understand the features and options to consider.

Can I book a fitting consultation?

Yes. Visit our Fitting Consultation page to learn about the process and request a consultation.

Do you provide insurance assistance?

We can provide general guidance about insurance documentation and financing options. Specific coverage depends on your provider and plan.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Include the type of equipment you are interested in, how it will be used, and any specific questions or requirements you already know about.

Can I ask about a product before purchasing?

Absolutely. Contact us with the product name or category and we can help explain the available options and features.
