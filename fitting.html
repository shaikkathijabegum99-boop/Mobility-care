Equipment Should Work For You

Choosing mobility equipment is about more than selecting the right product. Proper sizing, positioning, and adjustment can make everyday use more comfortable and easier to manage.

Our fitting consultation provides practical guidance based on the equipment you are considering and how you plan to use it.

  • Practical sizing guidance
  • Everyday-use considerations
  • Safe equipment setup guidance

PLEASE NOTE

Personalised Guidance, Not Medical Advice

Fitting consultations provide practical product and setup guidance. They are not a substitute for assessment, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional. Where appropriate, we recommend discussing your mobility needs with your healthcare provider.
