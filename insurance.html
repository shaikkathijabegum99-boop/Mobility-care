IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Coverage Can Vary

Insurance coverage, reimbursement rules, financing terms, eligibility requirements, and documentation requirements vary by provider, product, location, and individual circumstances. MobilityCare does not guarantee insurance coverage or financing approval. Please confirm specific coverage and terms directly with your insurance or financing provider.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Insurance & Financing Questions

Does insurance cover mobility equipment?

Coverage depends on your insurance provider, plan, product, location, and individual circumstances. We recommend confirming coverage directly with your provider.

Can you help me understand insurance paperwork?

Our team can provide general product and documentation guidance. Specific coverage and claim decisions are made by your insurance provider.

Are financing options available for all products?

Financing availability can depend on the product, provider, eligibility requirements, and applicable terms. Contact our team for current information.

Can I purchase a product without insurance?

Self-pay purchasing may be available for eligible products. Our team can help you compare products and understand pricing.

Can you help me choose the right product?

Yes. You can schedule a fitting consultation to discuss your mobility needs, product features, fit, and everyday use.
